FAQ

Before you order.

How do I order?

Use the website order form to send your request directly by email with your flavor choices, quantity, date needed, and pickup/event details.

Do you take custom orders?

Yes. Custom boxes, trays, and event quantities can be requested depending on availability and order size.

Where are you located?

H&H Cookie Co. is based in Roseville, Michigan. Pickup and event availability can be confirmed by message.

Do the cookies contain allergens?

Some flavors may contain or come into contact with dairy, wheat, eggs, peanuts, tree nuts, pistachio, and other common allergens. Ask before ordering if you have an allergy concern.
